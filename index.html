About Allan

How I Got Here

I grew up playing many sports, including baseball, basketball and football. Boxing came later, and I've been training and competing for over fifteen years. A lot of the same habits carry into how I work.

I studied computer science and started out building websites, running ads and managing social accounts for small businesses. Selling that work is what pulled me into sales, and I picked up everything that comes after a deal along the way: the quoting, the scheduling, the invoicing, the CRM.

So the three sides of what I do grew out of each other rather than being three separate careers. I've worked remotely for four years now, which means I run my own day and keep my own records. And I'm always partway through a book about doing some part of it better.
